# Voice sample (about 2 to 3 minutes)

The clone copies how you actually talk, not what you say. So most of this is you
answering questions out loud in your own words, like you're on a call with a
friend. Only the last part is read, and it's there for numbers and a question.

**How to record**
- Quiet room, no fan or music. Phone or laptop mic about a hand's width away.
- Voice Memos on the Mac or iPhone is perfect. Save it as `ME/voice-sample.m4a`.
- Don't perform and don't rush. Ums, restarts and "honestly" are fine; I'll trim
  long pauses.
- If one take feels stiff, just record the whole thing again. Takes are free.

---

## Part 1: talk (about 2 minutes, roughly 20 to 30 seconds each)

Answer these out loud, your way. Bullet points are only nudges, skip any of them.

1. **What are you working on right now?**
   (A-IEP, the evaluation stuff, the portfolio, whatever's actually on your mind.)

2. **Explain A-IEP to a friend who's never heard of it.**
   (Parents get a fifty-page plan, it turns it into plain language, their own language,
   names get stripped out first.)

3. **What happened with the Acharya app?**
   (You took it over, rating was 1.2, users kept growing, what broke, what you fixed.)

4. **Tell me about your setup or the last thing you played.**
   (PC builds, keyboards, Valorant, CS2. Whatever you'd actually ramble about.)

5. **A founder just joined the call. What do you tell them in the first thirty seconds?**

Read this casually. Change any word that you wouldn't say.
